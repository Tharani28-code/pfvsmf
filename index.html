Important: This tool gives estimates, not tax, legal or investment advice. Confirm the proposed PF cap and payroll treatment with HR/EPFO. Mutual-fund returns are not guaranteed. Inputs are processed in this page and are not uploaded anywhere.

How to interpret this

  • EPF and EPS are different. The employer contribution may be split between EPF and EPS. The EPS input is subtracted from the employer share before projecting the EPF corpus; EPS is shown separately and is not included in the EPF lump-sum projection.
  • Employee PF reduction is extra salary cash available because less is deducted from your pay.
  • Employer PF redirected is treated as extra taxable allowance in Option B only if your employer confirms that payroll treatment.
  • Tax is calculated separately for each scenario using the entered salary, bonus, standard deduction, and eligible employer NPS deduction. Employee PF contributions are not deducted from taxable income under the new regime.
  • Investment projection assumes month-end contributions and a steady return compounded monthly. Real returns fluctuate; mutual funds can lose money.
  • This simplified model does not fully calculate HRA exemptions, every employer contribution exemption/limit, surcharge and marginal relief for high incomes, capital-gains tax, fund expenses, inflation, or changes in law. It is best used for an initial comparison, not as a tax filing calculator.
